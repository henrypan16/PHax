Fax from your Windows PC, without a monthly fee

PHax sends and receives faxes through a USB fax modem and your existing phone line. Faxes never leave your office: no online fax service, no cloud account. Free and open source.

Download for Windows Ask a question

Windows 10 (1809 or later) or Windows 11, 64-bit. On the download page, click PHax-Setup-<version>.exe under Assets.

What it does

Send and receive

Fax PDFs, Word documents, images and scans, with cover pages. Received faxes land in an Inbox you can preview, print, forward or save as PDF.

Works in the background

A Windows service answers calls and sends the queue with automatic redial, even when nobody is signed in. It can print incoming faxes automatically.

Confirmed delivery

A fax is only marked Sent after the receiving machine confirms every page. Anything uncertain is flagged, never silently resent.

One modem for the office

One PC with the modem can fax for the other PCs on your network. They share the Inbox over an encrypted connection.

Scanning

Scan paper documents straight into a fax from WIA or TWAIN scanners.

Updates itself

Check for updates in Settings and install them in a couple of clicks. Your faxes and settings are kept.

What you need

A USB fax modem that supports Class 1. PHax is tested with the StarTech USB56KEMH2 (USB 2.0 56K V.92 fax modem, Conexant chipset). Other Class 1 modems may work but have not been tested yet. Class 2-only modems don't work.

  • A Windows 10 (version 1809 or later) or Windows 11 PC, 64-bit
  • An analog phone line, or a VoIP adapter port (turn on VoIP / digital phone line compatibility in PHax)
  • Other PCs that share the modem don't need their own

Get started

  1. Plug the modem into the PC and the phone line into the modem.
  2. Download PHax and run the installer. Windows SmartScreen may warn you because the installer is not code-signed yet: choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Open PHax, go to Settings → Modem, choose the modem's COM port and click Test connection.

Help and feedback

Questions and "does my modem work?" go to Discussions. Found a bug? Open an issue and attach Settings → Diagnostics → Export logs (the logs never contain fax pages).
